01 Digital twins in safety-critical industry UX research · HMI +
Why it matters

Misconfigured devices cost downtime and return visits; nobody downstream can see what was verified.

For the engineer

One surface instead of four: the panel, its warnings and the customer brief in the same place.

Impact

A traceable handover produced as a by-product of the work, not written from memory afterwards.

User research Industrial HMI Digital twin

Master's thesis with ABB: the digital twin of a motor control centre as the commissioning engineer's working surface, not a monitoring dashboard.

Read the whole panel at a glance — every device's configuration state on one topology
Warnings anchored to the device and slot that caused them, not a separate alarm list
A traceable handover produced by the work itself, so the next engineer knows what was verified

03 Domicile Rediscovered Installation · Exhibition +
Methods

Processing, crochet, projection mapping

Installation Projection Interaction design

Domicile Rediscovered is a journey through centuries of female resilience in Bosnia and Herzegovina. With a focus on emancipation, resistance, and empowerment, the piece reveals the methods women have used to display autonomy in a habitually patriarchal society. Traditional motifs and patterns are integrated into the project's visual language, drawing from the rich cultural heritage of the region. Additionally, the project emphasises the importance of traditional craft as a tool for expression and storytelling, highlighting women's strength and agency. The piece aims to amplify the voices of marginalized women and provide a platform for their stories to be heard and recognised within a broader social context.

About

I design interfaces for situations where the data is dense and the stakes are real. My training runs from interaction design at the Glasgow School of Art to interactive media technology at KTH, so I move comfortably between research, visual system and working prototype.

Most recently I researched digital twins in safety-critical industrial environments at ABB. Before that, five years of studio and agency work — brand identity, motion, projection mapping and interactive installations — taught me to ship under a client's deadline and explain a decision to people who do not speak design.

The creative side is not a footnote: I came into UX through exhibition and installation work, building interactive pieces that people walk into rather than click through — projection mapping, generative visuals, physical prototypes with Processing and Arduino, motion graphics and identity systems. That background is why my interfaces are composed as well as tested, and why I am as comfortable in Unity or P5.js as in a research plan. I am looking for a consultancy or product team where research and craft sit in the same pair of hands.
